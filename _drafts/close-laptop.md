A mild source of discomfort for me is people walking around with their laptop lid open.
I understand why: they want to resume their work at their destination right away.
They don't want to wait for their machine to wake up an login again.

There is a better way: you can close the lid, transport the laptop safely, and resume work at your destination without interruptions.
Here are the relevant settings on Windows, but there are similar ones on other operating systems.

Click on the battery in the notification area, then again on the battery.

{:refdef: style="text-align: center;"}
![](/assets/2026/laptop-open/open-battery-settings.png)
{: refdef}

Go to *Lid and power button controls* > set *Closing the lid will make my PC*  to *Do Nothing*.
Now your laptop will stay awake when you close it.

{:refdef: style="text-align: center;"}
![](/assets/2026/laptop-open/closing-lid-setting.png)
{: refdef}

You can still set your device to go to sleep after a while.
Then the battery won't drain if you leave your laptop on and closed.

{:refdef: style="text-align: center;"}
![](/assets/2026/laptop-open/go-sleep-battery.png)
{: refdef}

If your device doesn't go too sleep, by default you shouldn't need to login afterwards either.

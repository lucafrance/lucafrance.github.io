I am using Copilot in PowerPoint a lot an I have thoughts[^all-presentation-software].

[^all-presentation-software]: I mention PowerPoint here because it is what I am using at work, but the observations apply to every presentation software.

I have to use PowerPoint for work[^bad-medium]

[^bad-medium]: There is an argument to make whether PowerPoint is the right medium at all, but that is a topic for another blog post. In this one, I assume that a decision has been made to use PowerPoint slides as medium.

I like throwing shapes on slide in rough position of what I want and let Copilot figure out how to put it orderly.

I like referencing documents to do quality check.
E.g. Check that the information in the presentation is consistent with $attachment1, $attachment2, $attachment3.

I like that I can apply stylistic changes to the entire presentation quickly.

Copilot replaces work that previously often an intern would have done.

Text It maybe lacks personality, but it is fine.
Good enough and boring is good in business.

Why is it working.
If I tell Copilot to do analysis, in general it does not know whole context, therefore results are wild.
It can't know full information provided by customer interactions.
I might not be able to know what the information I collected I have, but it is the gut feeling.
E.g.: the customer doesn't care about that, this is not what the customer wants, the customer wants emphasis on this thing.
As long I do have to do the information collection in real life my job is safe.
I am a meat proxy for information from the real world to the machine.
Assuming that the machine has the full information, might be able to do a good enough job and one day replace me.



Copilot works very well if I provide an outline already structured with the information I want to present.

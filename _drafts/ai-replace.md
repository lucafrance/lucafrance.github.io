My job as a management consultant.
- Collect information about the organisation. Understand how the organisation works.
- Understand what can be improved in the organisation.
- Summarise few easy to follow recommendations that the humans realistically will implement.

The more context I provide (the less thinking the machine has to do), the better the machine response.

I use AI to reference information.

It is not good to provide recommendation directly.
It works better to give indication of recommendation.
E.g. based on $document-1, -2, -3, phrase reccomendation to establish service continuity management with consistent phrasing as in $document-for-standard-x

Balancing act to keep the high-value thinking to myself and delegate the low-value "thinking" to the machine.

Blog post separato su "Think"?
I often feel the instinct to delegate the thinking to the machine instead of thinking myself.
Acting without thinking was already issue before LLM.
Thinking is good (Technology connections quote).
Think! IBM slogan (che origine aveva)
Deep thinking Cal Newport.
Think! meme invincible.

Collecting information is not easily automatable.

I am writing a lot, to feed the information to the machine.

When I study the information and I use the AI, I can notice information which is unknowingly in my head and not in writing.

I can see that when information is better integrated, the machine can automate most if not all of my work.

[^not-my-customers]: I am obviously not talking about my customers. My customers are all great and work very efficiently.

An analysis is good when it feels obvious in hindsight.
